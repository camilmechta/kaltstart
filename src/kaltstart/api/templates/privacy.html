Privacy

Your data on Kaltstart

Who runs Kaltstart

Kaltstart is a non-commercial student project, in beta and by invitation. For any question about your data, write to {{ removal_contact }}.

If you have an account

Kaltstart keeps your e-mail address, your first and last name, your university, degree and programme, your languages, your target role, what you are looking for and when, your link if you add one, your preferred countries and sectors, your showcase sentences, and the mails you keep with their status.

It uses them to prepare your mails and to apply the limits that keep Kaltstart fair to companies.

You can delete your account at any time from your profile. Your account, profile and showcase sentences are removed at once. Mails you kept stay counted for the companies, without your name or their text.

If you work at a company in the list

Kaltstart keeps your name, your job title and the page of your company's own website where it was read, and the addresses your company publishes. Nothing comes from social networks or other websites.

A name is only a suggestion: a student must confirm it before using it in a mail.

It is removed when your company's last funding round is more than 12 months old, when your company's page no longer shows it, or as soon as you ask at {{ removal_contact }}.

What Kaltstart never does

Kaltstart never sends a mail: students send their own, from their own mailbox. It never sells or shares data, shows no ads, and has no visit statistics and no tracking. One cookie keeps you signed in, for 14 days.

Where the data is

The app runs on Render in Frankfurt, Germany. The database is on Supabase in Paris, France. Funding rounds come from the Tech.eu Funding Explorer, licence CC BY 4.0.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction or a deletion at {{ removal_contact }}. You get an answer within 30 days.

When the project ends

When the beta ends, all personal data is deleted. Only totals without any name are kept, such as how many mails received a reply.
