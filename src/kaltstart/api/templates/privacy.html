Privacy

Your data on Kaltstart

Who runs Kaltstart

Kaltstart is a non-commercial student project, in beta and by invitation. For any question about your data, write to {{ removal_contact }}.

If you have an account

Kaltstart keeps your e-mail address, your first and last name, your university, degree and programme, your languages, your target role, what you are looking for and when, your link if you add one, whether you attach a CV, your preferred countries and sectors, your showcase sentences, the mails you keep with their status and dates, and when your invitation code was used.

It uses them to prepare your mails and to apply the limits that keep Kaltstart fair to companies.

You can delete your account at any time from your profile. Your account, profile and showcase sentences are removed at once. Mails you kept stay counted for the companies, without your name or their text.

If you work at a company in the list

Kaltstart keeps your name, your job title and the page of your company's own website where it was read, and the addresses your company publishes. This list is read only from your company's own website, never from social networks or other websites. A student may also type a name or an address they found themselves; it is kept in that mail only. Kaltstart never guesses an address. For a LinkedIn message, Kaltstart keeps the texts and the person's name, as for a mail; it never visits LinkedIn.

A name from the list is only a suggestion: a student must confirm it before using it in a mail.

The list is updated when your company's page changes, and removed when your company's last funding round is more than 12 months old. If a student already kept a mail to you, your name and address stay in that mail until you ask or the student deletes their account.

You can ask to be removed at any time at {{ removal_contact }}. Only a fingerprint of your name is then kept, so that you are not added again.

What Kaltstart never does

Kaltstart never sends a mail: students send their own, from their own mailbox. It never sells or shares data, shows no ads, and has no visit statistics and no tracking.

One cookie keeps you signed in and protects forms; it expires 14 days after your last visit. Your light or dark choice is kept in your browser. To stop abuse, your IP address is counted in memory for at most an hour to limit sign-in and sign-up attempts, and is never stored.

Where the data is

The app runs on Render in Frankfurt, Germany, and pages pass through Cloudflare on the way. The database is on Supabase in Paris, France. Supabase, which handles sign-in, keeps its own technical logs of sign-ins, which can include your IP address. Funding rounds come from the Tech.eu Funding Explorer, licence CC BY 4.0.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction or a deletion at {{ removal_contact }}. You get an answer within 30 days.

When the project ends

When the beta ends, all personal data is deleted. Only totals without any name are kept, such as how many mails received a reply.
