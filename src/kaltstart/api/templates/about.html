About

A cold start, made warmer

Kaltstart helps students of the University of St. Gallen write to startups in Switzerland, Germany and Austria that have just raised money: the moment they are most likely to hire.

How it works

1. Pick a company. Every company in the list raised money in the last twelve months.

2. Choose who you write to. We suggest a person read on the company's own website, with the page we found them on, so you can check it. You can also pick someone else, type a name, or write to the team.

3. Keep your mail and send it yourself. Kaltstart writes a short mail around your showcase sentence. You copy it into your own mailbox. Kaltstart never sends anything.

Fair to founders

Each company receives at most 3 mails from Kaltstart students in 30 days, and each student writes to a company only once.

Where the data comes from

Funding rounds: Tech.eu Funding Explorer, licence CC BY 4.0.

Addresses and people: read on each company's own website only, from the pages it publishes (legal notice, team, contact). Never from social networks.
